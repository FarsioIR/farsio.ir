## Site baseline

## Query segmentation

### Quick wins

Queries with:

- position 4–15
- meaningful impressions
- low or average CTR

Action:

Improve title, snippet relevance, content answer block, and internal links.

### Page-two opportunities

Queries with:

- position 11–20
- clear product relevance

Action:

Expand topical coverage and strengthen internal links.

### Discovery signals

Queries with impressions but position >20.

Action:

Validate intent before investing in new content.

### Zero-impression strategic URLs

Do not immediately create more pages.

First verify:

- indexed?
- crawl date?
- sitemap recognition?
- internal links?
- intent demand?
- content quality?
